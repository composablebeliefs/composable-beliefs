- **"resolve"** — take a pointer-to-code and actually go find the spot it points at in the current files. **"validate"** — check the pointer is well-formed and lands on *exactly one* place (it warns if zero, or if many).
- **"bare `{path, anchor, nth}` stop rows"** — CB never stores line numbers (they rot the moment you edit a file).
